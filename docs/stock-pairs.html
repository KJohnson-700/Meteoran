Do DLMM LP gates, calibrated on memecoins, transfer to tokenized equity?

A pool-level cross-section of every Meteora DLMM pool quoting a verified equity token. 24,000 pools scanned, no wallet, read-only, standard library only.

the finding
Stock × memecoin pools pay an LP 4.6x lower median APR than stock-quoted pools.

Same equity beta in both — the only difference is what the other leg is. When the other leg is a memecoin, the fee share stops covering the variance. Pairing equity with something calmer is the trade, and it is measurable from public data without holding a position.

Data quality, and why it matters here. A large and moving fraction of the raw /pools pull is structurally corrupt — TVL of 3e-12, APR of 6e13. Every figure on this page is post-filter. A single unfiltered row sorts an entire table, which is why a public leaderboard built on the same endpoint can look precise and be meaningless.

What this is not

  • Not a strategy and not a bot. It places nothing and signs nothing.
  • Not a ranking product. No wallet-level skill measurement, no time series, no persistence test — one cross-section from one pull.
  • Not investment advice. It surfaces candidates and the conditions they trade under.
Figures come from Meteora's public DLMM API, pulled every 30 minutes by tools/stock_pairs.py; the page itself is rendered by tools/stock_report.py from data/stock_pairs.json. Every pool name links to its Solscan entry, so any number above can be checked against chain state. Reproduce with python3 tools/stock_pairs.py 60 && python3 tools/stock_report.py · source at github.com/KJohnson-700/Meteoran · 24,000 pools scanned, $631,557 TVL in the two classes above.
