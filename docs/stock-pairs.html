Why one window is not enough. A pool's 24h figure is an average over a day in which it may have been idle for most of it. On this pull the highest-24h stock pool paid AAPLx-USDC 1.949% over 24h against 0.000% over 1h — its yield had already fallen away by the time the day was done, and a 24h-only ranking would still have put it first. Conversely a pool that sat quiet all day and woke up an hour ago has no 24h volume at all, so a 24h-only view drops exactly the pool someone scanning right now wants to see. Both are shown, on every window.
Data quality, and why it matters here. A large and moving fraction of the raw /pools pull is structurally corrupt — TVL of 3e-12, yields of 6e13. Every figure on this page is post-filter. A single unfiltered row sorts an entire table, which is why a public leaderboard built on the same endpoint can look precise and be meaningless.

How these pools are classified

A pool is stock if either leg is a verified equity token — matched on mint family, issuer and ticker rather than on the pool's name, because pool names are user-supplied and one wrong ticker in an allowlist was enough to misclassify three live pools before this was caught.

stock × memecoin is the same test with a memecoin on the other leg. Both legs carry beta at once, which is why the two are tracked separately rather than pooled together.

What this is not

  • Not a strategy and not a bot. It places nothing and signs nothing.
  • Not a ranking of quality. No wallet-level skill measurement, no time series, no persistence test — one cross-section from one pull, so a pool's position here is a snapshot and not a track record.
  • Not investment advice. It surfaces candidates and the conditions they trade under. Every figure is here so it can be argued with.
Figures come from Meteora's public DLMM API, pulled every 30 minutes by tools/stock_pairs.py; the page itself is rendered by tools/stock_report.py from data/stock_pairs.json. Every pool name links to its Solscan entry, so any number above can be checked against chain state.
